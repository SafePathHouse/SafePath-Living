About SafePath Living

Our goal is to provide dependable housing in a welcoming environment where residents can feel comfortable and supported. We offer furnished living spaces, shared common areas and practical support options based on individual needs.

What We Offer

Designed to make independent living easier while maintaining dignity and personal independence.

24/7 Staffing

Staff support is available around the clock to help create a dependable living environment.

Furnished Rooms

Comfortable furnished rooms with utilities included and access to shared common areas.

Meals & Food Support

Meal preparation can be included, with assistance available for residents who need help obtaining a Bridge Card.

Transportation

Transportation options can be arranged to help residents access appointments, programs and daily activities.

Medication Reminders

Medication reminders and assistance can be provided when needed.

Behavioral Support

Behavioral support options are available, with day-program availability depending on insurance and eligibility.

Rooms Available

We currently accept serious inquiries regarding independent-living availability. Private-room availability may vary depending on current occupancy.
